🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 6, 2026

Sarkari Seva is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. What Information We Collect

Sarkari Seva collects minimal information:

  • Browsing Data: Pages you visit, how long you stay
  • Device Info: Browser type, device type (for mobile optimization)
  • Search Queries: What you search for on our site (for improving search)
  • Contact Form: Only if you submit the contact form (email, name, message)
  • PDF Tools: Files are processed locally in your browser - we do NOT upload or store them

2. What We Do NOT Collect

For your security, we do NOT collect:

  • Aadhaar numbers
  • PAN numbers
  • OTPs or security codes
  • Passwords or credentials
  • Bank account details
  • Credit/debit card information
  • Any personally identifiable government IDs

3. Cookies

Sarkari Seva uses minimal cookies:

  • We do NOT use tracking cookies
  • We do NOT use third-party advertising cookies
  • Technical cookies may be used for site functionality
  • You can disable cookies in your browser settings

4. PDF & Document Tools

For PDF merging, splitting, compression, and image tools:

  • All processing happens in your browser (client-side)
  • Your files are NOT uploaded to our servers
  • Your files are NOT stored
  • Your files are NOT scanned or analyzed
  • Files are automatically cleared when you close the page
  • This is why we can offer these tools completely FREE

5. Analytics

We may use basic analytics to understand site usage:

  • Number of visitors
  • Popular pages
  • Device types
  • General location (country/city level, not precise)

We only use this data to improve the website. Personal data is NOT stored in analytics.

6. External Links

Sarkari Seva links to official government websites:

  • These external sites have their own privacy policies
  • We are not responsible for external sites' data practices
  • Review each government website's privacy policy before using
  • Links open in new tabs to keep Sarkari Seva open

7. Contact Form

If you submit our contact form:

  • Your email and name are used only to respond to you
  • We will not add you to any mailing list without permission
  • We will not share your email with third parties
  • Messages are deleted after responding

8. Third-Party Services

We may use minimal third-party services:

  • GitHub Pages for hosting
  • Basic analytics tools
  • See their privacy policies at their respective sites

9. Security

We take security seriously:

  • The website uses HTTPS (encrypted connection)
  • We do not store sensitive personal data
  • We do not ask for passwords or credentials
  • Files in PDF tools are processed locally, not transmitted

10. Children's Privacy

Sarkari Seva does not knowingly collect data from children under 13:

  • If we discover we have collected data from a child, we will delete it
  • Parents/guardians concerned about data collection may contact us

11. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request what data we have about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of analytics
  • Disable cookies in your browser

To exercise these rights, contact us.

12. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary:

  • Contact form messages: Deleted after response
  • Analytics data: Retained for 12 months
  • PDF/tool files: Never stored (processed locally)
  • Browsing data: Used only for real-time analytics

13. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with an updated date. Your continued use indicates acceptance.

14. Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us.

15. Compliance

Sarkari Seva aims to comply with applicable data protection laws including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Personal Data Protection Bill (when applicable).
