📌 About Sarkari Seva

⚠️ Important Disclaimer: Sarkari Seva is an independent information platform and is NOT an official website of the Government of India, any state government, or any government agency. We are not affiliated with any government department.

What is Sarkari Seva?

Sarkari Seva is a free, non-profit information aggregation platform designed to help Indian citizens access information about:

  • Government job notifications and recruitment
  • Exam results and admit cards
  • Government forms and applications
  • Government schemes and benefits
  • Public government services (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, Passport, etc.)
  • Free document processing tools (PDF, image tools)

Our Mission

Our mission is to make government services and information more accessible to all Indian citizens by providing a centralized, easy-to-use platform with:

  • Direct links to official government websites
  • Clear, translated information
  • Free tools to help with document preparation
  • Mobile-friendly interface
  • Fast, reliable access

What We Do NOT Do

For your security and privacy, Sarkari Seva does NOT:

  • Collect, store, or process Aadhaar numbers
  • Collect, store, or process PAN numbers
  • Collect or process OTPs or security codes
  • Store passwords or government credentials
  • Provide fake government login forms
  • Bypass CAPTCHA or government security measures
  • Scrape or republish copyrighted government content
  • Upload your files to external servers (PDF tools work locally on your device)

Data Privacy

Sarkari Seva respects your privacy:

  • We do not store personal information
  • PDF and image processing happens entirely in your browser (no uploads)
  • We do not use cookies for tracking
  • We do not share data with third parties
  • See our Privacy Policy for details

Official Sources

We always link to official government portals:

  • Aadhaar/UIDAI: https://uidai.gov.in/
  • PAN/Income Tax: https://www.incometax.gov.in/
  • Voter Registration: https://voters.eci.gov.in/
  • Driving Licence: https://parivahan.gov.in/
  • Passport: https://www.passportindia.gov.in/
  • Government Schemes: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/
  • Main Government Portal: https://www.india.gov.in/

Who Runs Sarkari Seva?

Sarkari Seva is run by independent volunteers who believe government information should be easily accessible. We are not funded by the government and receive no special government access or data.
